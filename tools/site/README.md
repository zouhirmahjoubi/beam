# Palmlight

A hands-first mixed reality light puzzle for Meta Quest, made for the Meta VR Start Developer Competition 2026. Your desk becomes the board, and your open palm is one of the mirrors.

## Play

Open https://zouhirmahjoubi.github.io/palmlight/ in the Quest browser and press **Play in headset**. Put the controllers down, since the game is played with your hands.

To report speed, open https://zouhirmahjoubi.github.io/palmlight/?fps=1 instead. A small readout above the buttons shows the time per frame and the share of slow frames.

This repository holds only the built game.
